## Conflict Resolution
If files conflict:
1. factual source wins over stylistic source
2. finalized resume and LinkedIn content win over speculative website drafts
3. public copy must remain confidentiality-safe even if a more specific internal phrasing exists

## Working Rule
Before accepting website copy, check:
- factual accuracy
- confidentiality compliance
- tone consistency
- recruiter readability

## Key Approved Public Constraints
For Li Auto-related public copy:
- use `million-scale` or `1M+`
- do not include specific imbalance ratios
- use `tens of millions of RMB` only for a single vehicle model
- allowed metrics include:
  - ~30% life extension
  - ~85% efficiency improvement
  - 5 battery platforms
  - 3+ vehicle platforms
