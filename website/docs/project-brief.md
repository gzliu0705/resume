# Project Brief

## Project
Personal website for Guanzhang Liu.

## Purpose
Create a personal website that functions as a recruiter-friendly, narrative, editorial resume with a strong personal identity.

The website should help:
- recruiters quickly understand fit
- hiring managers understand depth and judgment
- peers sense working style and personality

## Approved Core Direction
- Core concept: **The Interpreter of Systems**
- Main narrative object: **a cat**
- Style balance: **70% professional / 30% playful**
- Visual direction: **2D flat, editorial, lightly playful**

## Phase 1 Scope
Phase 1 includes:
- one long-form homepage
- English only
- no blog yet
- no project detail pages yet
- no personal photo
- no heavy animation-first approach

## Homepage Goals
Within 30 seconds, a recruiter should understand:
- who Guanzhang is
- what kind of data scientist he is
- what key work he has done
- that he is open to London opportunities
- how to contact him

Within 3–5 minutes, a hiring manager or peer should understand:
- the trajectory of his work
- selected projects and why they mattered
- how he thinks
- what kind of collaborator he might be

## Phase 1 Deliverables
- documentation backbone
- source-of-truth policy
- homepage content files
- design planning docs
- implementation decision doc

## Constraints
- keep the site internationally legible
- avoid a too academic tone
- avoid a too China-local framing
- keep the homepage curated, not exhaustive
- maintain confidentiality-safe public phrasing
