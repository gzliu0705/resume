## E) Remaining Risks and Interview Mitigation

1. **No direct credit-domain experience.** Explain the common decision pattern: understand user behaviour, estimate risk or propensity, rank or target users, expose interpretable outputs, and monitor downstream outcomes.
2. **No explicit online experimentation.** Use the causal intervention project to demonstrate matched-control evaluation and counterfactual reasoning, while being clear that it was not a conventional randomised A/B test.
3. **No explicit multi-market deployment.** Position cross-platform normalisation and reusable feature definitions as adjacent experience, then explain how the same governance pattern would extend across markets.
4. **Dense one-page format.** The PDF is readable and ATS-compatible, but interview reviewers may focus only on the first two Li Auto projects and ByteDance. Those sections therefore carry the most role-relevant proof.

## F) ATS and Keyword Assessment

Strongly covered terms include Data Scientist, targeting, prioritisation, user behaviour, predictive modelling, machine learning, Python, SQL, production, monitoring, model lifecycle, stakeholder communication, feature engineering, and model evaluation.

Terms intentionally not forced into the CV include multiple markets, financial wellbeing, real-time personalisation, A/B testing, model optimisation, and online recommendation. They are either domain context or unsupported capabilities rather than genuine CV gaps to conceal.
