**Status:** Draft for the application form; not confirmed as submitted.

**Question:** Please introduce yourself. Tell us why you'd be perfect for RELEX and this position.

Hi, I’m Wentworth Liu. I’m a data scientist at Li Auto, where I work on battery-health forecasting and turning vehicle data into tools that other teams can use to make decisions.

I started in scientific research, which taught me to question assumptions and be honest about uncertainty. At ByteDance, I became interested in a more immediate challenge: helping a marketing team decide where to focus its efforts using imperfect, fast-changing data. At Li Auto, I’ve taken that further—projecting battery-health trajectories to identify at-risk vehicles, building reusable features across platforms, and helping bring a safety-ranking model into a monitored production workflow.

What connects these experiences is the question I enjoy most: how do you make a model useful beyond the person who built it? That’s why RELEX’s work on forecasting models for different customers appeals to me. I haven’t worked in retail forecasting, and I would have a new domain to learn. But I would bring experience in modelling, feature engineering and production delivery, along with a genuine interest in understanding the decisions a forecast needs to support. I’d be excited to bring that approach to RELEX.
